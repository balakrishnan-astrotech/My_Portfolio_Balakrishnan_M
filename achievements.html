Awarded first prize for a paper presentation at the 13th ISTE Annual Polytechnic Students Convention, hosted at Sankar Polytechnic College.

Paper title

Autonomous Hybrid Solar–EV Microgrid with AI Scheduling, Adaptive MPPT, PID Storage & SiC Conversion

Topic explanation

This paper presents a microcontroller-based, hybrid Solar–EV microgrid that maximizes solar utilization and reduces grid dependency. It integrates dual-axis solar tracking with adaptive MPPT and high-efficiency SiC-based DC–DC converters. A battery–supercapacitor hybrid storage system is managed via a dual-loop PID strategy, while an AI-based predictive scheduler optimizes power allocation across EV loads, storage, and the grid. Featuring optional IoT monitoring and self-healing fault detection, this scalable architecture delivers an intelligent, semi-autonomous, and practical energy management solution for next-generation power systems.
